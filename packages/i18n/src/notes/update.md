# update

- From `update.prompt.title`: The update dialog, shown when a new version is found. It lists what changed in that version under the `update.prompt.feature`, `update.prompt.fix` and `update.prompt.improvement` headings; the items themselves come from the release and are not translated.
- `update.prompt.versions`: `{version}` and `{current}` are version numbers such as 2.0.11.
